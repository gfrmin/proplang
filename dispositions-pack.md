### II.1 Issue #18 — stdout block-buffered under pipes

**Confirmed at HEAD** (probe above), and by source: no
`hSetBuffering`/`hFlush` anywhere in app/Main.hs or
src/PropLang/Host.hs (the loop reads at Host.hs:454). A plain
`stdin=PIPE, stdout=PIPE` host deadlocks on the first hello; every
downstream host is forced onto a PTY for what is one buffering line.

**Builder recommendation: fix-it, as a transport micro-increment.**
Oracle-first: a pipe-transport oracle suite that spawns the built
binary over plain pipes and asserts per-line reply delivery (the probe
above is its prototype); then `hSetBuffering stdout LineBuffering`
plus a per-reply flush in the IO layer (app/Main.hs or Host.hs's loop —
gate-3-legal either way, the polling loop already lives in Host.hs).
Zero semantics, zero alphabet, zero prices. The one design note: the
oracle must drive the *installed executable*, not the library — a new
shape for a suite, but the probe demonstrates it is a dozen lines of
process plumbing.


Every probe row in the issue is what this code predicts: `var 0` is
constant across candidates, ties resolve first-listed under `cv > bv`,
and per-candidate reads work only through `Get` (a candidate's
assignments are features during its evaluation — `feats ++ a`, the
step-6 geometry). This goes to Part III as frozen-layer inventory
item 1, with the repair drafted. The alternative — *implementing* the
option-code binding — is a semantic wire change buying capability
`get` already provides, and is not recommended.

**(c) The accepted-but-inert guard on a writable name.** Probe 15b:
a guard on a menu name is accepted at hello and inflates the model
count (1169 → 1241 — a live hypothesis dimension), yet can never
receive evidence (D-b2 rejects any tick carrying a writable name as a
feature) and never affects the act. An accepted declaration with no
reachable consequence is the fail-closed doctrine's missing case.
**The question for the author:** reject guard-on-writable-name at
hello (doctrine-consistent, the builder's recommendation — as a row in
whichever wire micro-increment next opens), or document the inertness
as intended. Note the rejection is a behavior change for any world
that currently declares one (it would get `bad hello` where it got
`ok`), which is why it is a ruling and not a fix.
