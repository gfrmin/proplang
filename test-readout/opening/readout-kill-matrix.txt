READING, for the sitting.
  M72 IS UNREACHABLE BY EVERY STANDING ROW. That is the whole point of
  the r8 group: on a single-point menu the chosen act and the menu head
  are the same assignment, so no row written before r8 can tell a
  head-computed vector from a chosen-act one. r8 earns its seat.
  r8c and r8d shadow EACH OTHER against M72, and that is STRUCTURAL
  SHADOWING, an answer rather than a failure: they differ only in the
  DIRECTION of a test-side assertion (the chosen act's vector IS on the
  wire; the head's is NOT), and a test-blind src mutant cannot separate
  them. Whether the pair is wanted is a row-VALUE ruling for the
  author, never a pool obligation.
  r8a and r8b run UNREACHED and EARNED-nothing respectively; both are
  GUARD rows, not claims about the readout. r8a pins the fold point the
  reference assumes and r8b pins that chooseEU's head fallback did not
  quietly decide the row. Deleting either turns r8c/r8d into rows that
  pass on assumptions - which is the failure mode they exist to close.
  Every cell's standing corpus is GREEN, so every kill above is
  READOUT-UNIQUE.
